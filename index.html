💌 Dear Lovelove nako,

Sorry kaayo palangga nako.

Kabalo ko naka himo kug sala and I only hope you'll take a moment to read this and understand how truly sorry I am.

You mean so much to me, and it hurts knowing that my actions caused you pain. I never wanted to be the reason behind your tears or sadness. If I could take back the mistakes I made.

I promise to learn from my mistakes and become a better person, not just for us, but because you inspire me to be better every day. I know trust and forgiveness take time, and I won't force you to forgive me. I'll do my best to prove through my actions that my apology is sincere.

No matter what happens, I want you to know that I truly love you. You are one of the greatest blessings in my life, and I hope we can heal together and create happier memories in the future.

I love you so much, Lovelove. I'm sorry, and I hope you can find it in your heart to forgive me.

Forever yours,
Adrian ❤️
